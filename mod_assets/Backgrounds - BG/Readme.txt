Another Re-Design but this time for Backgrounds! There are several categories to view

	City - Assets themed in a City

	House - Assets themed in a house or a room

	Residential - Assets themed in the surburban area of DDLC

	School - Assets themed at the Doki School

	Misc - Assets that are un-characterized

Inside there are options to find that one asset you need based on what room, weather/sun setting, POVs and effects.
Here is a basic guide for some category options

	DDLC Theme - Assets that used the Monika Space Room color

	Blender Ver - The Blender Version of the Classroom and Corridor

	Paranormal - Something looks...off...

	Dark - Not night-time but a dark effect

	POVs - Different camera angles of the scene

	Gloom - Not Paranormal but very cloudy gray

	Meme Themed - Contains items that are memes

	Transparent - Assets that contain no background
